BRAND SCHEDULER

One calendar for every owned brand.

Brand Scheduler is a private web application used by Aiviq Enterprises Inc. to connect authorized social accounts, prepare short-form videos, schedule publication, and retain publishing-status logs.

The service is limited to accounts owned or managed by Aiviq Enterprises Inc. Account access is granted through each platform's official authorization flow and can be revoked at any time.
