BRAND SCHEDULER

Privacy Policy

Effective: August 22, 2026

Brand Scheduler is a private social media scheduling service operated by Aiviq Enterprises Inc. for social accounts that it owns or is authorized to manage.

Information we process

  • Account identifiers, display names, profile images, and authorization tokens supplied through official platform authorization flows.
  • Videos, images, captions, schedules, and publishing settings submitted by authorized users.
  • Publishing results, platform post identifiers, error messages, and security or operational logs.

How we use information

We use this information only to connect authorized accounts, schedule and publish requested content, display publishing status, maintain security, troubleshoot failures, and comply with applicable law and platform rules.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the social platform selected for publication and with infrastructure providers needed to operate the service. We do not access unrelated third-party accounts or use platform data for advertising.

Retention and security

Authorization tokens are protected and retained only while an account remains connected. Content and publishing logs are kept only as long as needed for scheduling, audit, security, or legal purposes. Access is restricted to authorized personnel.

Your choices

Platform access may be revoked through the applicable platform settings. Requests for access, correction, deletion, or other privacy questions can be sent to admin@aiviq.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
